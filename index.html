Freehold

Your data stays where it is. The question comes to it, and only the answer leaves.

The idea

Everything the internet sends you arrives as it is. Everything you tell a company about yourself is gone the moment you send it.

Freehold turns both around, with a Hub at your door that works for you.

In

Coming in

What comes in gets checked at your door, on your rules, so the good things reach you first.

Out

Going out

A company sends its question to your Hub. Your Hub works out the answer at your place and sends back only that answer.

Label

The sealed note

Every answer carries a signed label: who worked it out, who may read it, what it may be used for, and when it stops counting.

The settings dial

You set the dial for both directions. Off is allowed, and it always will be.

The name

You share the harvest and keep the field.

A freehold is land you hold outright, owing nothing to anyone. You share what it produces. The ground stays yours.
