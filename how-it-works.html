The ecosystem and the wall socket

We build the Freehold Insights Ecosystem, in which your data stays where it is. The question travels to it, the algorithm runs on the spot, and only the answer leaves.

Three examples of the shape
Three banks

Together they establish whether a payment is suspicious, without any one of them seeing another's customer file.

A hospital and a research group

They reach an answer about a treatment while the patient records never leave the hospital.

A machine builder

It warns the owner that servicing is due without taking possession of that factory's production data.

One comparison runs through the whole page, the electricity grid. A hundred years ago the same thing happened there: a line was laid, one socket became the standard, and after that thousands of other people built appliances for it. We think data now stands at that point.

The connection point and the ecosystem

The electricity grid never succeeded through the grid alone. It succeeded because one standard arrived and one socket, after which anyone was free to build an appliance for it.

The device already exists
At your home or in the business there is a connection point in the ecosystem, and a Hub runs on it. It runs as a working prototype under the name Semek, it sits next to your router, and today it checks photos, videos and text without anything leaving your home. Freehold's Products lead is developing it, and it is the device the Hub will run on. See semek.ai.

The governance of register and tariff

Whoever determines which algorithm is admitted, and whoever determines which traffic runs at cost, exercises authority over everyone else building in the same ecosystem. Both decisions belong to a board on which Freehold does not sit by itself, with published conditions and with a right of appeal against a refusal.

SIX

The break points of the comparison

The picture of the electricity grid carries far, and it does not carry everything. Below is where it stops.

The form of the return

With us what you get back is first of all the better offer itself.

The incomparability of answers

Two kilowatt hours are identical and therefore tradable on an exchange. Two answers never are.

The absence of a monopoly

A grid operator holds a regulated monopoly. We do not.

The free alternative

Everyone is on the electricity grid because there is no alternative. For data that alternative does exist, namely the internet as it works today.

The run-up of electrification

In 1899 three per cent of American homes had electric light. It took about four decades after the first central station before half was reached, and in Great Britain five per cent of houses were wired in 1919.

The name and the patent application

Freehold is the name of what we build. A freehold is land held in full ownership, as against feudal tenure, where you hold land from a lord to whom you owe service and dues.
